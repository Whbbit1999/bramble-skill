## Knowledge-graph frontier

Track only the next few ready or nearly-ready capabilities. Do not turn the plan into a rigid exhaustive syllabus.

## Review rhythm

Use adaptive retrieval checkpoints rather than rereading-only review. Persist active retrieval work in the review queue when supported.

## Error-driven adaptation

Recurring foundational errors may insert a repair lesson or alter the order of later phases.

## Progress rule

Advance based primarily on demonstrated capability, delayed retrieval, and transfer — not merely elapsed time or content consumed.

## Dynamic replanning rule

Regenerate the next lesson when evidence changes prerequisite readiness, exposes a recurring misconception, shows that a planned lesson is redundant, or reveals that the learner is ready for transfer/integration.
